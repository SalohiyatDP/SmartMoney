**2. Trading Sessions**:
- **Asian Session** (00:00-09:00 GMT):
  - Range trading
  - Low volatility
  - Liquidity pool shakllanishi
  
- **London Session** (08:00-17:00 GMT):
  - High volatility
  - Major moves
  - Liquidity sweeps
  
- **New York Session** (13:00-22:00 GMT):
  - High volume
  - Trend continuation/reversal
  - News impact

**3. 5-Bosqichli Strategiya**:
1. HTF market structure aniqlash

**Qanday Foydalanish**:
